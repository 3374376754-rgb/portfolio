Press Release · July 28, 2025

Autel and Shanghai AI Laboratory Open the "Physical AI" Track, Accelerating AI's Move Into the Real World

SHANGHAI — The World Artificial Intelligence Conference wrapped up its three-day run on July 28, drawing the global AI community around the theme "Intelligent Era, Shared Future." Among the week's most closely watched announcements was a new partnership between Autel, a robotics and smart-hardware innovator, and the Shanghai AI Laboratory, one of the world's leading AI research organizations.

Together, the two organizations officially opened the "Physical AI" track — a global competition and open collaboration platform designed to move artificial intelligence out of the cloud and into the physical world. The initiative invites researchers and engineering teams worldwide to tackle the hardest problems in embodied intelligence: spatial understanding, multimodal reasoning, and multi-agent coordination in real-world environments.

"We are at an inflection point where AI is no longer confined to screens," said Li Hongjing, founder of Autel. "Physical AI is the frontier, and no single organization can build it alone. This partnership is an open invitation to the best minds on the planet to join us."

The track arrives as the industry shifts its focus from model performance to physical deployment, echoing NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang's widely cited framing — "the next wave of AI is physical AI" — systems that can perceive, reason, and act in the real world, from autonomous robotics to intelligent hardware for energy and transport.

On the exhibition floor, Autel showcased its air-ground embodied intelligence solution — a coordinated system of aerial and ground robots driven by a shared "AI brain" that fuses multimodal perception with on-device reasoning. The company positioned the platform as an open foundation for industrial customers across energy, transport, and logistics.

The collaboration also marks Autel's entry into the research platform's founding partner ecosystem, anchoring a long-term commitment to turning research breakthroughs into deployable, commercially viable systems.

"We look up at the stars, but we also keep our feet on the ground," Li Hongjing added. "Our goal is simple: to turn the promise of physical AI into real solutions for the industries that keep the world moving."

Shifting the narrative from a "copycat dispute" to a "tech duopoly" (finance edition).

[ CONFIDENTIAL ]
致：[ 创作者名称 ]
合作主题：中国硬科技出海的"双雄时代"
内容形式：1 × 深度财经分析视频（Bilibili / 抖音 / 视频号）
Objective

引导受众从"情绪化的抄袭争议"，转向"中国影像科技企业如何通过良性竞争做大全球蛋糕"的宏观思考。不直接洗白，而是用商业逻辑降维打击负面舆论。

Tone & Creative Direction
  • 基调：客观、硬核、深度、数据驱动。
  • 画面要求：多用市占率图表（如全球全景相机 / 无人机市占率折线图）、专利数据可视化图表、海外市场营收对比，避免使用带有强烈负面情绪的网友评论截图。
Talking Points
  • Hook（切题）："最近科技圈都在吃两家巨头的瓜，但如果你只看到'抄袭'，那就太小看中国硬科技出海的残酷与伟大了。"
  • The Pivot（升维）："其实，这不是简单的侵权，而是一场典型的'专利护城河'与'品类创新'的碰撞。回顾苹果与三星、耐克与阿迪达斯，任何一个成熟的行业，最终都会走向'双寡头（Duopoly）'的格局。"
  • The Logic（客观分析）："一方是无人机霸主向下兼容做全景相机，另一方是全景相机王者向上探索做无人机。表面是互偷老家，内核其实是在各自的舒适区之外，用极高的研发投入去试探对方的底线，最终把整个行业的门槛拉高，让海外老牌厂商无路可走。"
  • The Conclusion（升华）："神仙打架，凡人遭殃？不，神仙打架，才能逼出最强的中国制造。比起内卷价格，我更希望看到这两家中国公司，在海外市场上顶峰相见。"
Do's and Don'ts
  • DO：强调"竞争带来创新"、"专利战是出海必修课"、"两家都在疯狂砸钱做研发"。
  • DON'T：绝对不要在视频中站队，不要使用"偷"、"山寨"等定性词汇，不要提及任何具体的负面黑公关传闻。
  • DON'T：避免煽动两家粉丝的饭圈化对立，评论区需引导为"理性探讨产业格局"。
